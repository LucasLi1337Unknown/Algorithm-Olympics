HONEST RULEBOOK

Dead ends. No cheat exits.

Each seed generates sixteen connected, acyclic candidates using randomized growing-tree carving. We pick the highest score for exit-route length, turns and dead ends. This creates winding routes and traps; it is not a proof of maximum difficulty.

One round advances each active contestant by one cell visit. Planners count expansions, including repeated expansions in iterative deepening; walkers count occupied cells, including their initial cell. Frontier scans, priority bookkeeping and CPU time are not scored.

A* uses Manhattan distance and g + h. Dijkstra and BFS are equivalent on these unit-cost trees. Greedy searches and custom priorities are not universal shortest-path algorithms; here all solutions share the one valid route.

Lolz names describe custom experimental policies built from familiar search ideas, not claims of new scientific discoveries. Random walking and iterative deepening may hit their explicit budgets. BUDGET STOP does not mean escaped.
